Section 1
Privacy Policy
Short version: Nammade Bus does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information about you. We don't know who you are. 😊

What data we collect

Nammade Bus stores the following information only on your device using browser localStorage — it never leaves your phone:

Data Purpose Stored Where
Selected stop name Remember your boarding point Your device only
Destination name Remember your destination Your device only
Theme preference (light/dark) Remember display preference Your device only
Pinned journey details Track your current journey Your device only

What we do NOT collect

We do not collect your name, phone number, email address, location, device ID, IP address, browsing history, or any other personal information.

Firebase Database

We use Google Firebase Realtime Database to store bus schedule overrides (cancellations, replacements). This database contains only bus schedule information — no user data whatsoever.

Third Party Services

Nammade Bus is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect standard server logs including IP addresses as part of normal web hosting. Please refer to GitHub's privacy policy for details.

Children's Privacy

Nammade Bus does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The app is a public transport schedule tool suitable for all ages.

Disclaimer
Important: Bus schedules shown in this app are approximate and may not reflect real-time conditions. Always allow extra time for your journey! 🚌

Schedule Accuracy

The bus timings displayed in Nammade Bus are based on the standard schedule for the Poomala–Thodupuzha route. Actual arrival and departure times may vary due to:

Traffic conditions, road closures, weather conditions, mechanical issues, driver changes, festivals and public holidays, strikes or bandhs, or any other unforeseen circumstances.

No Official Affiliation

Nammade Bus is an independent community project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to KSRTC, Kerala State Government, or any bus operator on the Poomala–Thodupuzha route.

Limitation of Liability

The developer of Nammade Bus shall not be held responsible for any inconvenience, loss, or damage arising from reliance on the schedule information provided in this app. Users are advised to verify schedules independently for critical journeys.

Service Availability

Nammade Bus is provided free of charge on a best-effort basis. We do not guarantee uninterrupted availability of the app or its features.
